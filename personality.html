Personality

Claude reads this before every message, so it decides how your pet talks and what it cares about. Changes save automatically and apply on the next chat.

What can I put here?
  • Voice: how it talks (length, tone, sounds, emoji).
  • How it feels: how food, mood and energy should color replies.
  • Work buddy: how it motivates you, what to remind you about.
  • Anything else: likes, running jokes, your name, your projects.
